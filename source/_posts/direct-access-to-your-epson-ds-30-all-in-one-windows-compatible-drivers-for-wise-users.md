Here’s how:

1. Click **[here](https://epson.com/Support/Printers/All-In-Ones/WorkForce-Series/Epson-WorkForce-WF-7710/s/SPT%5FC11CG36201?review-filter=Windows+10+32-bit)**  to visit the download webpage of Epson.
