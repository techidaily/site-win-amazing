**1)** Go to the **[Logitech official website](https://tools.techidaily.com/drivereasy/download/)**  , then click**SUPPORT > Downloads** .

**2)**  Type **k400 plus**  on the search box, then select **Wireless Touch Keyboard K400 Plus** .
